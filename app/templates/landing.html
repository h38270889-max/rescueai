Workflow Architecture

How ResQAI Coordinates Relief

A resilient 4-stage pipeline connecting affected people with certified rescue coordinators.

1. Report in Any Language

Submit incident reports online or offline. Geolocation coordinates and scene photos are packaged locally with an idempotency key.

2. AI Emergency Triage

Intelligent rule and LLM classification instantly determines incident category, estimates urgency (Critical to Low), and provides immediate survival steps.

3. Responder Coordination

Authorized field responders receive prioritized queues with mapped GPS coordinates, original text, and accurate translations.

4. Targeted Broadcasts

Emergency commanders dispatch high-priority geographical and category-specific broadcast warnings to all consented users.
