Academic & Engineering Overview

ResQAI Platform Architecture

Diploma in Computer Engineering Final Year Project: AI-Powered Multilingual Emergency Triage and Offline Disaster Support System.

Project Mission

During large-scale floods, cyclones, and earthquakes, cellular networks degrade and central call centers experience severe call congestion. Non-native speakers encounter critical language barriers when describing injuries, and victims with limited battery life or spotty network coverage cannot wait on hold.

ResQAI bridges this gap with an Offline-First Progressive Web App paired with Zero-Latency Deterministic Rule Triage and pluggable Large Language Models (Google Gemini). It allows affected citizens to report hazards in 14 languages, queues drafts in client-side IndexedDB, and synchronizes securely with emergency coordinators as soon as intermittent connectivity flickers back.

Ethical & Safety Guardrails
  • Auxiliary Platform: ResQAI does NOT replace official telecommunication emergency numbers (112, 100, 101, 102).
  • No Hallucinated Commitments: The system never tells a user that a rescue team has arrived unless confirmed by an authorized coordinator.
  • Strict Medical Scope: AI triage does not diagnose illnesses or recommend prescription medication; it provides basic first aid guidance and urges professional care.
